shorts-otomatis

A private publishing tool for one YouTube channel.

shorts-otomatis is a private, single-user tool run by the owner of this website. It uploads short videos that the owner produces to the owner's own YouTube channel, schedules when they go live, and reads that channel's own analytics to decide which topics to make next.

The tool is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and never accesses anyone else's YouTube account or data.
